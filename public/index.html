Frequently asked questions

Does this need internet or a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Just open the file — no server, no internet required.

How accurate are the estimates?

The model achieves R²=98.5% on training data. Treat results as a guide, not a formal valuation.

What do the locality tiers mean?

Every area is grouped into premium (tier 0), mid-range (tier 1), or affordable (tier 2). If you type a place not in our list, an average rate is used.

What is the price unit?

All prices are in Indian Rupees — shown in Lakhs (₹ X Lakh) or Crores (₹ X Cr) automatically.
