How it works

Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the estimates?

Accuracy depends on the training data. The R² score above shows how much of the price variation the model explains. Treat results as a guide, not a valuation.

What do the localities mean for price?

Every city and area is grouped into a premium, mid-range or affordable tier. If you type a place we do not list, an average small-city rate is used.

Can I use my own data?

Yes. Place a data.csv next to the server (sqft, bedrooms, bathrooms, age, tier, price) and restart; the model retrains automatically.
